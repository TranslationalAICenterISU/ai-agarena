AI-AgARENA: Advancing AI for Climate-Smart Agriculture

A national, multi-site AI-ready testbed enabling cutting-edge research in cyber-agricultural systems for sustainable farming, climate resilience, and food security.

'AI-ready Cyber-Agricultural Systems Testbed (AI-AgARENA)' seeks to develop an AI-ready cyber-agricultural system that enables AI research for climate-smart agriculture by integrating autonomous farm management, continuous real-time sensing of soil and plant conditions and weather parameters, and intelligent data harvesting for analysis and visualization. AI-AgARENA is a multi-state, multi-site AI-ready farming testbed that integrates and enhances existing agricultural research platforms—complemented with wireless networking, sensors, ground robots, and drones—to develop and validate AI tools for cyber-agricultural systems.

About AI-AgARENA

A Multidisciplinary Collaboration for Agricultural Innovation

Our Team

AI-AgARENA brings together a diverse, multidisciplinary team of experts including plant scientists, agronomists, AI specialists, roboticists, wireless communications experts, cyberinfrastructure and cybersecurity professionals, designers, extension staff, education experts, and entrepreneurs from three leading research universities.

Foundation Projects

Our team leads several major federally funded projects that provide the foundation for AI-AgARENA:

  • COALESCE: NSF CPS Frontier grant on sustainable agriculture focusing on context-aware learning for cyber-agricultural systems
  • AIIRA: National AI Institute for Resilient Agriculture at Iowa State University
  • ICICLE: National AI Institute at Ohio State with significant agricultural applications focus
  • ARA: NSF PAWR platform providing 5G-and-beyond wireless living lab connectivity to agriculture farms
  • CyVerse: Scalable cyberinfrastructure platform for collaborative data-driven discovery

Testbed Infrastructure

AI-AgARENA integrates cutting-edge physical and cyber infrastructure across multiple sites, covering diverse crops, geographies, soils, and climates.

Physical Infrastructure

Iowa State Core Facility

40+ acres of dedicated agricultural research farm with comprehensive capabilities:

  • Ground robots (Amiga) with 360° cameras
  • Hyperspectral, thermal, and multispectral imaging
  • 440V power substation and solar hybrid systems
  • 5G connectivity through ARA wireless living lab

ISU Digital Ag Innovation Lab

A globally unique living lab conducting production system research on 1,000+ acres annually:

  • High technology readiness level (TRL) testing facility
  • 35+ annual contracts with private industries
  • 90+ patents and technology transfer licenses
  • 60+ fully commercialized AgTech products

UA Field Scanner

World's largest and most advanced automated phenotyping system:

  • 30-ton robotic gantry system
  • Generates 2-6 terabytes of data per day
  • High-quality camera and sensor suite
  • Open-source control software for custom sensor integration

UA Yuma Farms

500+ acres for diverse crop research:

  • Valley Farm (274 acres): cotton, grains, vegetables
  • Mesa Farm (240 acres): citrus production
  • 140-mile fiber network via Sun Corridor Network
  • 26 wireless towers enabling county-wide smart farming

OSU Molly Caren Center

2,100-acre facility for corn, soybeans, and wheat:

  • Private 4G network (upgrading to 5G by Spring 2026)
  • Download speeds: 150 Mbps, Latency: 60ms
  • Support for vision-based autonomous vehicles and drones
  • Focus on computational approaches for yield, resilience, sustainability

Cyberinfrastructure

ARA Wireless Living Lab

First-of-its-kind wireless connectivity for precision agriculture:

  • Coverage: 60+ km diameter across ISU campus, Ames, and rural areas
  • Low-UHF massive MIMO to mmWave wireless access
  • Long-distance backhaul and free-space optical communications
  • Low-earth-orbit (LEO) satellite communications

CyVerse Infrastructure

Integrated cyberinfrastructure for data-driven discovery:

  • Federated data store for managing edge device and sensor data
  • Kubernetes-based Discovery Environment with GPU support
  • Data workbenches (Jupyter) and workflow managers (Argo, Nextflow)
  • AI-VERDE: Metered API access to commercial and open-source LLMs

Shared Software & Data Practices

Software:

  • Infrastructure as Code (IaC): Terraform and Ansible templates
  • ML/AI tools: MLFlow, Ray, DVC, Datachain
  • Frameworks: PyTorch, JAX, TensorFlow
  • CACAO platform for easy template management

Data:

  • Croissant standard for machine-readable metadata
  • Discoverable via Google Dataset Search
  • FAIR and CARE standards compliance
  • AR/VR visualization for immersive data exploration

Data & Model Governance

Comprehensive governance framework:

  • Strict authentication and authorization enforcement
  • Audit and tracking tools for data and model usage
  • Enhanced tagging for improved data discovery
  • Link validity checking and duplicate detection

Get Involved with AI-AgARENA

Join our growing community of researchers, students, industry partners, and stakeholders shaping the future of AI-driven agriculture.

AI Researchers

Access our multi-site testbed for developing and validating AI algorithms and tools. Leverage state-of-the-art sensing platforms, robotics, and data infrastructure for your research projects. Join our network of guest researchers beta-testing cutting-edge agricultural AI applications.

Agricultural Scientists

Collaborate on research integrating domain expertise with advanced AI capabilities. Access comprehensive data collection, analysis tools, and visualization platforms to advance plant breeding, crop management, and sustainable farming practices.

Students & Educators

Participate in internships, coursework, and hands-on training programs. Engage with Farmverse VR learning experiences. Access educational resources and workshops spanning K-12 through graduate levels, building skills for the future agricultural workforce.

Industry Partners

Collaborate on technology development, testing, and commercialization. Access facilities for product validation and stakeholder demonstrations. Partner on research projects ranging from prototypes to high-TRL commercial applications in AgTech innovation.

Farmers & Extension

Participate in guided demonstrations of climate-smart farming technologies. Provide feedback on practical applications and real-world needs. Engage in extension programs designed to improve adoption of AI-driven agricultural practices.

Government & Policy

Partner with us to ensure AI-AgARENA provides valuable tools for the farming community while maintaining data privacy and security. Collaborate on developing safe AI research practices and guidelines for agricultural technology deployment.

Planning Workshops & Community Engagement

We regularly organize workshops, seminars, and webinars to engage the broader AI and agricultural communities. Join our mailing list and participate in upcoming events to help shape the future of AI-AgARENA and contribute your expertise to this national effort.
